About

I am a psychiatric social worker. I hold a PhD in Social Work from M.G. University, Kottayam, and an M.Phil in Psychiatric Social Work from the National Institute of Mental Health & Neuro Sciences (NIMHANS), Bangalore.

Since 1996 I have worked as a personal and family counsellor in private practice. Since 2018 I have been the full-time Student Welfare Officer at RSET in Kakkanad, after serving in the same role part-time from 2008.

Languages English, Malayalam and Hindi
